About Me
A little bit about who I am

I'm a 11 grade Master's student in Data Science with a deep interest in coding, ethical hacking, and the stories that data can tell. I believe research is fundamentally a human endeavor — driven by wonder and shaped by rigour.

My thesis explores natural language processing for regional languages, with a focus on making AI more inclusive for underrepresented communities. Outside the lab, I enjoy reading, mentoring junior students, and long walks that help me think through problems.

I'm actively seeking research internships and collaborative projects where I can contribute meaningfully and continue learning from inspiring people.
